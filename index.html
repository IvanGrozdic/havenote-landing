Havenote started from a simple frustration: journaling apps make you type, task apps make you type, list apps make you type, and it's hard to think clearly while typing on a phone. I wanted one thing I could just talk to, in whatever language I happened to be thinking in.

Havenote's creator

Talk. Havenote listens.
